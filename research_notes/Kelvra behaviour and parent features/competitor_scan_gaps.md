# Competitor Scan: Parent, Behaviour, Answer-Practice, Confidence and Wellbeing Features (and Kelvra's White Space)

Scope note: research done 2026-11-05 under a 15-search limit. Many sources are vendor pages or aggregators, flagged where relevant. Several apps named in the brief (Unacademy, Toppr, Doubtnut, Testbook, Adda247, Allen digital, Vedantu parent features, Google Family Link, Seesaw, Grammarly for Education, Cerego, Area9, Anki) were **not verified** in this pass and are listed under Gaps, not described from memory.

## 1. Indian edtech: what parents get and what they complain about

### Takeaway
Indian parent features are **dashboards of activity and test scores** (attendance, lectures watched, DPP or test stats, batch rank, purchase or payment management). None of the verified ones explain *why* a child is struggling, report on habits or wellbeing, or give the student any say in what parents see. The biggest parent-trust problem in the category is sales behaviour (BYJU'S), not product features.

### Cited Findings
- **BYJU'S Parent Connect**: shows a child's progress and performance from the BYJU'S Learning App and Maths App, flags "subjects that need more attention", and gives "suggested actions for each subject depending on progress and performance of the child in each chapter." The iOS listing was last updated Aug 26, 2021, and an Android aggregator marks it "Removed", so it looks stale or discontinued. — [AppFollow iOS listing](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/byju-s-parent-connect/1223316815?country=in); [chrome-stats Android listing](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.byjus.parentappv2)
- The same aggregator's user-feedback summary says data-sync problems are reported often and make progress tracking inaccurate (aggregator summary, not primary reviews). — [chrome-stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.byjus.parentappv2)
- BYJU'S FAQ: every child has a progress report, reached by switching profile or through the "Parent Zone" inside the student app. — [BYJU'S FAQ](https://byjus.com/faq)
- **PW Parent App (Physics Wallah)**: a dashboard of the child's attendance, lecture, DPP and test stats; performance within their batch and course; alerts for upcoming classes, tests, assignments and events ("No need to depend on your child for updates"); switching between several children; viewing purchased courses and managing payments and subscriptions; a support or query section. Requires iOS 14+, versions 1.0.9 to 1.1.0 with bug-fix-only notes. — [App Store: PW Parent App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pw-parent-app/id6478144871)
- **Embibe for Parents**: "keep an eye on everything the child learns", real-time learning-progress updates and syllabus-completion tracking. Marketing copy says parents can "take control of conversations in parent-teacher meetings" (no mechanism described). — [Embibe parent page](https://www.embibe.com/in-en/parent/)
- **BYJU'S sales controversy (2022)**: NCPCR summoned BYJU'S CEO after reports that sales staff lured parents into buying courses. The NCPCR chair alleged BYJU'S was buying phone numbers of students and parents and threatening them. — [Inc42: NCPCR summons](https://inc42.com/buzz/child-rights-panel-summons-byjus-ceo-for-alleged-malpractices); [Inc42: buying phone numbers](https://inc42.com/buzz/byjus-buying-phone-numbers-students-parents-threatening-ncpcr-chief); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/education/byjus-founder-to-appear-before-ncpcr-as-it-probes-claims-of-parents-lured-to-buy-courses/1277048)
- Context (Thomson Reuters Foundation) spoke to 22 customers, several from low-income homes, who said they were aggressively targeted, with some coerced or tricked into taking loans. BYJU'S said it does not incentivise selling to people who cannot pay and only connects parents to third-party lenders. — [Context](https://www.context.news/socioeconomic-inclusion/indian-tech-firm-byjus-hauled-in-by-watchdog-over-context-expose)
- In response, BYJU'S announced "affordability checks" and said it would stop selling to families earning under Rs 25,000 (reported threshold). — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/sector/ed-tech/byju-s-will-stop-selling-courses-to-lower-income-families-report-news-6967)

### Inferences
- Indian parent apps are built for **surveillance plus upsell**: attendance, test stats, batch rank and payment management sit side by side in PW's app. They are one-way and ignore the student's view.
- No verified Indian parent app reports on study *habits* (consistency, focus quality), *confidence*, or *wellbeing*, and none gives the student control over what is shared.
- After the BYJU'S episode, a public "no sales calls / no loan offers" pledge and transparent pricing could build trust with Indian parents (this is an inference, not a tested claim).

### Gaps
- No verified data on the parent features of Unacademy, Vedantu (PTMs), Allen digital, Toppr (believed to have been absorbed into BYJU'S, but not verified here), Doubtnut, Testbook or Adda247. Their app store listings and help centres need checking.
- No primary app-store review analysis (star ratings, review themes) for PW Parent App or Embibe.
- The outcome of the NCPCR probe was not found.

## 2. Global apps: parent dashboards, AI tutors, behaviour and focus design

### Takeaway
Global leaders have moved to **Socratic or "guided" AI tutoring** (Khanmigo, ChatGPT Study Mode, Gemini Guided Learning, all 2025) and simple parent dashboards (Khan). Behaviour design runs either on **streak or loss-aversion gamification** (Duolingo), criticised as anxiety-inducing, or on **friction-based blocking** (Opal, one sec, Forest). Classroom behaviour points (ClassDojo) carry a substantial body of academic criticism around surveillance.

### Cited Findings
**Khan Academy / Khanmigo**
- The 2025 **Parent Dashboard** gives "one clear view of their growth: learning minutes, skills practiced, and lessons completed". Parents can track progress and assign practice. — [Khan Academy blog: 2025 updates for parents](https://blog.khanacademy.org/2025-khan-academy-updates-every-parent-should-know/); [BTS 2025](https://blog.khanacademy.org/need-to-know-bts-2025/)
- Paid Khanmigo plans can enable Khanmigo for up to 10 child accounts, and parents get Khanmigo tools on the Parent Dashboard. — [Khan support](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/ka/articles/21823169137037)
- **Writing Coach** supports brainstorming, outlining and drafting "without doing any of the writing for them". Pricing is about $4/month. — [Khan blog: Writing Coach](https://blog.khanacademy.org/?p=16775)
- Claim that parents can see chat history: third-party only, unverified. — [aitoolsatlas](https://aitoolsatlas.ai/tools/khanmigo)

**ChatGPT Study Mode and Gemini Guided Learning (2025)**
- OpenAI launched **Study Mode** on July 29, 2025 (Socratic questioning, can be toggled mid-chat, quizzes and flashcards, free tier included). Google launched **Guided Learning** in Gemini about a week later (images, diagrams, videos, interactive quizzes, free). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/06/google-takes-on-chatgpts-study-mode-with-new-guided-learning-tool-in-gemini/); [Pearson explainer](https://pearson.com/international-schools/international-schools-blog/2026/02/what-is-gemini_s-guided-learning-and-chatgpts-study-mode-.html); [Digit comparison](https://www.digit.in/features/general/googles-guided-learning-versus-chatgpts-study-mode-which-is-better.html)

**Duolingo**
- Streaks act as the core motivator, using "questionable design patterns" to keep them alive (UX critique). — [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/the-good-the-bad-and-the-ugly-of-duolingo-gamification-3a12f0e80dc7)
- Parenting guides say guilt-style owl notifications can cause "genuine digital anxiety" in sensitive kids. Source is a monitoring-app vendor, so it is biased. — [ScreenWise](https://screenwiseapp.com/guides/managing-duolingo-streaks-when-gamification-becomes-stressful)
- The Family plan (Super) is about $9.99/month for up to 6 people versus $6.99 individual, and reportedly removes the Hearts penalty (single source). — [ScreenWise Duolingo guide](https://screenwiseapp.com/guides/duolingo-and-the-streak-obsession)

**Focus and distraction apps**
- **Forest**: you grow a virtual tree during a focus session (it dies if you leave), plus a phone lockdown mode. **Opal**: app and website blocking on iPhone and Mac, plus "Deep Focus" with friction (break requests that take time) so blocks are hard to bypass. Opal was built iOS-first on Apple's Screen Time API and its Android app lags behind. **one sec**: an intervention or pause *before* a distracting app opens. — [Opal vs Forest (Opal's own blog)](https://opal.so/blog/opal-vs-forest); [aisotools comparison](https://aisotools.com/compare/forest-vs-opal-screen-time); [boreme comparison](https://boreme.pages.dev/boreme-vs-one-sec-vs-forest)
- Opal's Android changelog renamed "Difficulty" to "Resistance" (Standard and Increasing modes). — [APKMirror](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/opal-the-focus-company/opal-screen-time-limit-timer/)

**ClassDojo (behaviour points) and its criticisms**
- Manolev, Sullivan and Slee (UniSA), in "The datafication of discipline" (Learning, Media and Technology), argue ClassDojo intensifies and normalises surveillance of students, creates a culture of performativity, and "reduces the complexity of behaviour to a number". Teachers used it to make students visible, sometimes combined with exclusion as punishment. — [Taylor & Francis paper](https://informahealthcare.com/doi/full/10.1080/17439884.2018.1558237); [EurekAlert press release](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/732702); [EducationHQ](https://educationhq.com/news/classdojo-harms-teachers-behaviour-management-approach-researcher-188825/)
- Critics note that public point displays shift students' focus to points rather than growth, and they raise data and privacy concerns. — [EdTech Books: five critical questions](https://edtechbooks.org/jaid_13_2/technology_for_good_or_evil_asking_five_critical_questions_of_classdojo)
- Counterpoint: Bahçeci (2019) found the app effective at increasing positive behaviours in tenth graders. The evidence is small-scale on both sides. — [EdTech Books](https://edtechbooks.org/jaid_13_2/technology_for_good_or_evil_asking_five_critical_questions_of_classdojo)

**Discontinued or changed (2025)**
- **Quizlet Q-Chat** (AI tutor) was discontinued after June 30, 2025. Quizlet shifted to AI Learn mode, Quick Summary and Magic Notes (aggregator source). — [Quizgecko](https://quizgecko.com/ar/blog/best-q-chat-alternative)
- **Google Socratic**: the standalone app was retired and its functions folded into Google Lens (reported 2025, aggregator sources, website status unclear). — [Wikiwand: Socratic](https://wikiwand.com/en/articles/Socratic_(Google)); [nibble-app](https://nibble-app.com/blog/socratic-app)
- **Brainly** relaunched in 2025 with an AI layer over its community Q&A database. — [fast.io review](https://fast.io/resources/brainly-ai-review-2026/)
- **Photomath**: no discontinuation found and still active per 2026 reviews. — [unstar.app](https://unstar.app/blog/tag/quizlet)

### Inferences
- General-purpose AI tutors (Study Mode, Guided Learning) are now **free**, so "AI mentor that asks Socratic questions" alone no longer sets an app apart. Kelvra has to win on exam-specific structure, memory (mistake notebook, SRS) and the family layer.
- Focus apps block apps but are unaware of the *syllabus or plan*. Study apps know the plan but don't manage distraction. Nobody verified joins the two.
- Behaviour-point and streak models both carry documented harm risks (surveillance, anxiety), so there is room for "behaviour insight without leaderboards or public scoring".

### Gaps
- Google Family Link, Seesaw, Grammarly for Education, Anki and Cerego features were not verified in this pass.
- No primary Duolingo statement on streak design or Family plan behaviour (e.g. a family streak or leaderboard) was retrieved.
- No independent study of Forest, Opal or one sec effectiveness was retrieved.

## 3. Long-form or descriptive answer evaluation (CBSE, UPSC Mains) and spoken or viva practice

### Takeaway
AI answer evaluation for **UPSC Mains** is a busy niche of small vendors (PrepAiro, AnswerWriting/Anthroholic, PadhAI, Dalvoy and others) with **unverified accuracy claims and no independent validation**. For **CBSE**, AI grading tools are sold to institutions, not students, and CBSE officially denies using AI to mark board papers. Spoken-answer practice exists almost only as **job or placement interview** apps. No verified product offers school or exam **viva** or "explain-it-aloud" practice for Indian syllabi.

### Cited Findings
- **PrepAiro** says it returns Mains feedback in about 60 seconds, calibrated on 10,000+ human-evaluated answers for GS 1-4. No validation is published. — [PrepAiro mains evaluation](https://prepairo.ai/upsc/features/mains-evaluation/)
- A vendor-linked blog claims a "+23% in 30 days" score boost with no methodology. Other vendor pages concede AI "cannot fully replicate a UPSC examiner's judgment" and "struggles with nuance, subtlety, and contextual depth." — [Anthroholic: AI vs human](https://answerwriting.anthroholic.com/ai-answer-evaluation-vs-human-evaluation); [Anthroholic: generic vs dedicated](https://answerwriting.anthroholic.com/generic-ai-tools-vs-dedicated-upsc-answer-evaluation-platforms-what-actually-improves-your-score); [UPSC eval blog: limitations](https://upscanswerevaluation.blogspot.com/2025/12/upsc-mains-answer-evaluation-ai.html)
- PadhAI got press for an AI scoring 170/200 on UPSC Prelims 2024. That was MCQs, not Mains writing. — [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/amp/story/national/ai-app-scores-170-out-of-200-in-upsc-prelims-2024)
- **CBSE (May 2026)** said AI was *not* used to evaluate Class 12 sheets. Its On-Screen Marking scans sheets for human examiners. Students can now get photocopies of evaluated sheets before verification or revaluation. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/ai-was-not-used-cbse-defends-digital-marking-amid-class-12-result-row-531878-2026-05-17); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/trending/cbse-osm-students-need-not-panic-class-12-board-exams-not-checked-by-ai/)
- Institutional AI grading (e.g. **Eklavvya**): OCR on scanned handwritten sheets, LLM evaluation against a model answer, and a draft score for faculty to review and override. It is pitched to coaching institutes and universities. — [Eklavvya](https://www.eklavvya.com/ai-grading-coaching-institute/); [Eklavvya OSM auto-evaluation](https://help.eklavvya.com/a/osm-auto-evaluation/)
- Spoken practice, all interview or placement focused: InterviewReady (freshers, technical and HR rounds), CareerClutch (voice, video and text with scores), VoiceCoach AI (group discussion practice, real-time speech feedback, Hinglish mode), TalkDrill (English speaking for HR rounds), and a Kochi startup's AI interviewer. The closest "viva" product is InterviewBoss (Bangladesh, Bangla feedback). — [TalkDrill comparison](https://www.talkdrill.com/blog/compare/best-apps-for-interview-practice-english/); [CareerClutch Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.prepai); [Cloudvyn list](https://www.cloudvyn.com/blog/free-ai-mock-interview-platforms-india-freshers-2026); [Onmanorama](https://onmanorama.com/career-and-campus/top-news/2024/08/05/startup-kochi-ai-based-job-seekers.html)
- Eklavvya also offers an AI mock interview (institutional). — [Eklavvya help](https://help.eklavvya.com/a/ai-mock-interview/)

### Inferences
- A student-facing, **rubric-transparent** answer evaluator for CBSE or state-board descriptive answers (marking-scheme steps, keywords, diagram or labelling checks) appears not to exist as a consumer product. It is an institutional B2B tool today.
- UPSC evaluators compete on speed and claimed calibration. None verified publishes agreement with human examiners, shows calibrated confidence in its own score, or tracks how a student's specific writing weaknesses change over time.
- "Explain aloud" (Feynman-style) or viva practice tied to *syllabus content*, as opposed to job interviews, is an open space, especially in Hindi, Hinglish and regional languages.

### Gaps
- No independent accuracy study (e.g. AI versus human examiner agreement) of any Indian answer-evaluation tool was found.
- Not checked: whether Testbook, Unacademy, Vision IAS, Drishti IAS or similar offer human or AI Mains evaluation inside their apps.
- No Indian app offering CBSE practical or viva practice was found. That was one search only, so absence is likely but not certain.

## 4. Confidence and metacognition features

### Takeaway
Confidence-based answering is established in **corporate and medical training** (Amplifire's patented Confidence-Based Learning) but **missing from consumer and Indian exam apps** in this scan. No verified Indian or global consumer study app asks "how sure are you?" and uses the answer to target "confidently wrong" misconceptions.

### Cited Findings
- **Amplifire** asks the learner to answer and then rate confidence (framed as certainty, doubt or ignorance). It compares confidence with correctness to find "confidently held misinformation" and adjusts the learning path. Dashboards sort by displayed confidence. — [Amplifire CBL explained](https://amplifire.com/?p=2584)
- Amplifire holds a patent for analytics on learner confidence. — [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/amplifire-secures-patent-for-analytics-regarding-the-confidence-of-learners)
- An academic preprint, "Learning with Confidence" (arXiv, Aug 2025), is a related research thread. Not reviewed in depth. — [arXiv 2508.11037](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.11037v1)

### Inferences
- Pairing every quiz or flashcard answer with a one-tap confidence rating ("guess / unsure / sure") and feeding *confidently wrong* items into the mistake notebook with priority would be rare in the consumer and exam market. Check the scope of Amplifire's patent before copying its exact analytics.
- Anki-style self-grading (Again / Hard / Good / Easy) captures *recall effort*, not confidence before the answer is revealed (general knowledge, not verified here).

### Gaps
- Cerego and Area9 Rhapsode confidence features were not verified in this pass.
- No evidence was found either way on whether any Indian app (Embibe and others) collects confidence ratings.

## 5. Wellbeing features for exam students

### Takeaway
Wellbeing support for Indian exam aspirants is mostly **offline** (yoga, helplines, counsellors in Kota) or comes from **separate mental-health apps** (LISSUN, Solh). No verified study or exam-prep app weaves wellbeing (stress check-ins, burnout signals, sleep, workload) into its daily plan or parent view.

### Cited Findings
- LISSUN launched a platform for exam-prep students, presented in Kota. Outcomes are vendor-claimed. — [education21](https://education21.in/lissun-adds-tech-platform-to-support-mental-health-of-students-during-exam-prep/)
- Solh/Streffie: daily stress check-ins with tailored coping strategies (vendor blog). — [Solh blog](https://www.solhapp.com/blog/student-suicides-kota-resilience-technology)
- IIPH Gandhinagar's "Conquer Exam, Be a Warrior" app, built with the National Mental Health Programme (Gujarat), targets students *and* parents and assesses exam fear, life satisfaction and burnout. — [uLektz news](https://news.ulektz.com/?p=32011)
- Kota institutes responded to student suicides with yoga and Zumba, 24x7 helplines and on-campus counsellors. — [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/amp/story/national/yoga-zumba-classes-mental-wellness-in-focus-as-student-suicides-shake-kota-s-factory-settings--news-247147); [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/coaching-in-kota-students-fight-homesickness-stress-amid-cut-throat-competition-measures-taken-by-authorities)
- Rank and batch comparison is a core parent-facing feature in PW's parent app ("how they are performing in their respective batches"). — [App Store: PW Parent App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pw-parent-app/id6478144871)

### Inferences
- Indian parent apps push rank comparison to parents, and wellbeing tools sit in separate apps. A study app whose plan adapts to stress and sleep signals, and whose parent report covers effort and wellbeing (with student consent) rather than rank, would be differentiated.
- Any wellbeing feature for 14-17 year-olds needs crisis escalation (Tele-MANAS 14416 and others) and careful framing. This is a design inference, not sourced here.

### Gaps
- No independent evaluation of any Kota or edtech wellbeing feature was found.
- India-specific regulatory guidance (e.g. the Ministry of Education 2024 coaching-centre guidelines) was not retrieved in this pass.

## Gaps / White Space for Kelvra (synthesis)

These are inferences from the findings above. Each is "not covered well by any app verified in this scan."

1. **Consent-based, two-way parent view.** Indian parent apps (PW, BYJU'S Parent Connect, Embibe) are one-way monitoring of attendance, scores and rank. A student-controlled sharing model (the student chooses what parents see, e.g. weekly effort plus plan adherence, not raw scores or chat logs) is missing. This matters more for the 17-24 age range, where surveillance would put users off.
2. **"Why, not just what" parent reports.** Parents get stats (tests, DPPs, minutes) but no plain-language explanation of *causes* (e.g. "careless errors in units", "concept gap in electrostatics", "studying late and skipping revision") or *how a parent can help*, in Hindi or a regional language. BYJU'S "suggested actions" came closest and looks discontinued.
3. **Confidence-tagged answering for exam students.** Confidence-based learning exists only in enterprise (Amplifire). A consumer exam app that captures "how sure are you?" on every quiz or flashcard, flags *confidently wrong* items into the mistake notebook, and shows a calibration trend is white space.
4. **Student-facing, marking-scheme-aware descriptive answer feedback for board exams.** CBSE-style AI grading is B2B (Eklavvya) and CBSE itself doesn't use AI. UPSC tools give opaque scores with no published accuracy. A photo-upload evaluator that shows *which marking-scheme points were earned or missed*, states its own uncertainty, and tracks recurring writing weaknesses over time is open.
5. **Syllabus-linked spoken answer and viva practice.** Spoken AI practice is almost entirely job-interview oriented. "Explain this concept aloud" or board-practical viva practice with feedback on correctness, structure and clarity, in English, Hindi or Hinglish, has no verified competitor.
6. **Focus tools that know the study plan.** Forest, Opal and one sec block distraction but don't know the syllabus. Study apps know the plan but ignore distraction. Linking a focus session to a specific plan item or mistake-notebook review, then reporting *quality* of focus rather than just minutes, is unclaimed.
7. **Anxiety-safe habit design.** Duolingo streaks (guilt notifications) and ClassDojo points (public scoring, surveillance) carry documented criticism. "Forgiving" consistency mechanics (weekly targets, streak freezes by default, no public leaderboards, no guilt copy) could be a clear brand position.
8. **Wellbeing built into the daily plan.** Wellbeing for Indian aspirants lives in separate apps or offline. A plan that adjusts load from short check-ins (sleep, stress, burnout signals) and escalates safely (helpline links) is not offered by any verified study app.
9. **No-dark-pattern, no-sales-call trust promise.** The BYJU'S NCPCR episode (bought phone numbers, pressure selling, loans) left a trust deficit with Indian parents. An explicit "no sales calls, no loans, cancel anytime" commitment is a differentiator few competitors can credibly claim.
10. **A mistake notebook that the parent and mentor both understand.** No verified app turns a student's mistake log into a shared, non-judgemental artefact (e.g. "3 recurring mistakes this week and the fix plan") for a parent or mentor conversation, replacing rank-based PTM talk.
11. **Free general AI tutors raise the bar.** ChatGPT Study Mode and Gemini Guided Learning (both free since mid-2025) commoditise Socratic tutoring. Kelvra's defensible layer is exam-specific memory: SRS, mistake notebook, plan, marking schemes, confidence data and the family layer. Generic chat is not.

Caveat for the report writer: absence claims are based on this limited scan. Unacademy, Vedantu, Allen, Testbook, Adda247, Doubtnut, Family Link, Seesaw, Grammarly, Cerego and Area9 were not verified and could partly cover some gaps (especially #2, #3 and #6).
