# Paying B2B / B2B2C niches in India a solo developer could serve with a focused AI product (as of Nov 2026)

Research method note: about 35 web searches, India-only. Most pricing comes from vendor pages, review aggregators (Capterra, Techjockey, GetApp) and competitor blogs; these are flagged where used. I could not open inc42.com directly (host blocked in this session's network settings), so Inc42 material is from search snippets only. No Reddit/Quora threads or Play Store reviews surfaced in search, so first-hand tutor/teacher voice is a gap throughout. Items older than 2025 are marked **[pre-2025]**. "Unverified" means a single vendor/competitor source with no independent check.

## 1. Education sellers: home tutors and small coaching centres (market size, workflow, existing tools, what Classplus-type platforms fail at)

### Takeaway
Private tuition is huge and government-measured (27% of all school students take coaching; Ministry-cited industry value above ₹58,000 crore), but the two best-funded "SaaS for tutors" companies both moved away from small tutors: Classplus toward its Testbook test-prep business, Teachmint toward selling classroom hardware to schools. The remaining offer for a 30-student tutor is either a ₹20,000-32,000/year branded-app platform that is oversized for them, or a swarm of near-identical free/₹99-999/month fee-and-attendance trackers; nobody credible owns "content work" (tests, worksheets, checking, parent reports) for the small tutor.

### Cited Findings
**Demand side (how many students, how much they pay)**
- Government CMS Education Survey 2025 (NSS 80th round, Apr-Jun 2025, 52,085 households, 57,742 students): 27.0% of students took private coaching in the current academic year; 30.7% urban, 25.5% rural. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/27-of-students-rely-on-private-coaching-urban-participation-higher-cms-education-survey-2025)
- Average annual household spend on coaching per student: ₹3,988 urban, ₹1,793 rural; all-India ₹2,409 across levels; Delhi ₹5,643 with 39.1% participation. Tripura 78.6% participation, followed by West Bengal and Odisha. Spend rises roughly four-fold from primary to higher secondary. — [Careers360 Delhi piece](https://news.careers360.com/cms-education-survey-2025-four-in-ten-delhi-school-students-take-tuition-spend-twice-the-national-average); [Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/1092310/coaching-classes-private-schools-are-increasing-education-expenses-in-india-in-six-charts); [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/cms-education-survey-2025-one-in-three-students-opt-for-private-coaching-urban-households-spend-significantly-more)
- Industry size estimates conflict: (a) ~₹58,088 crore now, projected ₹1,33,995 crore by 2028 (Infinium Global Research, a paid-report consultancy) — [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/theweek/business/great-indian-tuition-boom.html) (attribution to this exact page not verified; figure surfaced via search summary); (b) GST collected from coaching rose from ₹2,240 crore (2019-20) to ₹5,517 crore (2023-24) **[pre-2025 data]**, implying only ~₹30,000 crore of GST-paying (i.e. formal, above-threshold) coaching revenue — [Factly](https://factly.in/data-gst-revenue-from-coaching-industry-more-than-doubled-between-2019-20-and-2023-24); (c) IMARC: USD 7.2 billion in 2025 — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-coaching-institutes-market).

**Supply side (how many tutors/centres)**
- No official count of tutors or coaching centres was found. Proxies: UrbanPro claimed 6.5 lakh registered tutors/teachers/institutes in 2018 **[pre-2025]** — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2018/03/urbanpro-connects-6-5-lakh-verified-tutors-teachers-institutes-25-lakh-students); a competitor's 2026 page cites "over 7.5 lakh listed tutors" on UrbanPro and notes listed is not active (unverified) — [FindMyGuru](https://www.findmyguru.com/compare/findmyguru-vs-urbanpro). Classplus claimed to have digitised 1 lakh tutors serving 12M students in 2021 **[pre-2025]** — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2021/04/startup-kunal-shah-sequoia-blume-classplus-digitise-tutors-coaching).

**Getting new students: tutors already pay for leads**
- UrbanPro charges tutors per lead through "coins": plans from about ₹590-625, ~36-40 coins deducted per student contact; consumercomplaints.in lists 57 complaints alleging fake leads and non-refunded coins (complaint-site data, unverified). — [FindMyGuru](https://www.findmyguru.com/compare/findmyguru-vs-urbanpro); [ConsumerComplaints](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/bycompany/urbanpro-a404395.html)

**Existing platforms and prices**
- Classplus: third-party listings show Starter ₹19,999/yr + GST, Grow ₹22,999/yr, Advanced ₹24,999/yr, top tier ₹31,999/yr + GST; another listing ₹29,999/yr (down from ₹34,999). Figures conflict and are not verified against the vendor. — [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/software/208852/class-plus); [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/021852/classplus)
- A 2026 review says Classplus "pricing may feel high" for smaller centres/individuals when many features go unused; its fit is small-to-mid institutes planning to scale, and the branded app is what people pay for. — [GeekChamp](https://geekchamp.com/classplus-pricing-reviews-2026/)
- Classplus FY24 **[pre-2025 data]**: Entrackr reports operating revenue ₹213 crore (2x) with loss cut 57%; Inc42 reports a far lower standalone figure (₹73.9 crore revenue) and says Testbook was ~64% of consolidated revenue. Unreconciled. — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/2024/10/classplus-revenue-spikes-2x-to-rs-260-cr-in-fy24-cuts-losses-by-57); [Inc42 feature, July 2025 (snippet only)](https://inc42.com/features/from-enabler-to-aggregator-classplus-flips-its-edtech-playbook/)
- Inc42's July 2025 feature is titled "Classplus Flips Its Edtech Playbook: Test Prep Vertical Outshines Early SaaS Bet" and, per a competitor's summary of it, the Testbook acquisition, Polaris offline school and GyanDhan investment drew attention away from the core coaching SaaS. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/from-enabler-to-aggregator-classplus-flips-its-edtech-playbook/); [Edmingle blog (competitor)](https://www.edmingle.com/blog/classplus-alternatives)

- A secondary analysis (personal notes site, low authority) states small tutors churned heavily from both, moving to free Zoom + Google Classroom. — [deepaksood619 notes](https://deepaksood619.github.io/education/competitors/lms-platforms/india/classplus-teachmint-analysis)
- Edmingle: priced on monthly active learners; "Power" plan cited at ₹29,999/month (own blog) vs $349/month (Capterra) — aimed at training businesses, not tutors. Graphy India: 10% revenue share + GST per transaction plus ₹19,999 one-time onboarding (per Edmingle citing Graphy's help centre; competitor source). — [Edmingle blog](https://www.edmingle.com/blog/lms-pricing-in-india/)
- Small-tutor fee/attendance trackers: Cledgr free / ₹999 / ₹2,499 per month; TeachDesk free up to ~15 students then ₹499/month; MemberFlow from ₹99/month (still "testing phase"); TutorTrack free up to 20 students (beta). Several are hosted on vercel.app / netlify.app / pages.dev subdomains, i.e. solo-developer side projects. — [GoodFirms Cledgr](https://www.goodfirms.co/software/cledgr); [TeachDesk](https://teachdesk-web-project.vercel.app/blog/best-free-app-tuition-fees-attendance-india); [MemberFlow](https://memberflow.pages.dev/coaching); [TutorTrack](https://gettutortrack.vercel.app/)

**Test/worksheet creation tools and prices**
- Examin8 (CBSE test generator): yearly packs from ₹500 for 20 papers (₹25/paper) up to ₹10,000 for 1,000 papers; white-label Android app licence ₹3,000/year plus ₹50 per student per subject per year for premium content; blog also mentions ₹10 per paper with solutions. — [Examin8](https://examin8.com/); [Examin8 FAQ](https://examin8.com/blog/faq-on-test-generator-packages/); [Examin8 docs](https://docs.examin8.com/)
- PrepAI: from ₹19 per user per month (Capterra listing). — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/231360/PrepAI/)

**Fee collection**
- Vendors describe the standard small-centre stack as Excel sheets, registers and WhatsApp reminders; WhatsApp-automation vendors claim 35-50% faster/on-time collection, with no independent study. — [Jodo](https://www.jodo.in/blog/insight/skilling-institute-in-2025/); [AiSensy blog](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-for-coaching-institute-or-tuition-center/)

### Inferences
- Willingness to pay exists at two proven price points: ~₹500-3,000/year for content utilities (Examin8) and ~₹20,000-32,000/year for a branded app (Classplus). There is a visible hole between them: a ₹3,000-10,000/year "teaching assistant" for the 20-150 student tutor.
- Funded players exiting the small-tutor segment is double-edged: it shows the segment is hard to make venture-scale (high churn, low ARPU, expensive field sales) but that is exactly the profile a solo developer with near-zero costs can live on. 300 tutors x ₹6,000/yr = ₹18 lakh/yr.
- The "fee tracker / attendance app for tutors" idea is saturated with hobby clones and has a free substitute (UPI + WhatsApp). Avoid leading with it.
- Tutors already pay for student acquisition (UrbanPro coins) and resent lead quality. A product that helps a tutor win and retain students (branded weekly parent report, demo-test-to-admission funnel on WhatsApp) sells against a line item they already fund. The reader's WhatsApp and lead-gen experience fits here.
- State concentration matters: West Bengal, Tripura, Odisha, Delhi have far higher tuition participation; Bengali/Odia/Hindi-medium, state-board-aligned content is where national tools (CBSE-first) are weakest. (Based on CMS state figures above.)

### Gaps
- No reliable count of home tutors or coaching centres in India; every number is a platform's self-reported registration figure.
- No Classplus FY25 financials, paying-customer count, churn rate or renewal-complaint data found. Capterra shows 0 user reviews. Play Store reviews not retrieved.
- Wise (wise.live), MyClassCampus, Testbook-for-institutes pricing: nothing found.
- No survey on how small centres actually create papers (typing/DTP costs) or check sheets; needs 10-15 direct interviews.

## 2. School teachers and low-fee private schools (lesson plans, blueprint-aligned papers, HPC report cards, CBSE changes; who buys)

### Takeaway
There are ~3.4 lakh private unaided schools with ~39.6 lakh teachers, and 2026 brought real new workload (two Class 10 board exams, ~50% competency-based questions, on-screen marking, holistic progress cards). But schools buy as institutions at ₹50-500 per student per year from a very crowded ERP field, and no evidence was found of individual Indian teachers paying for tools. For a solo developer this is a slower, more crowded sale than tutors.

### Cited Findings
- UDISE+ 2024-25 (via secondary compilation of official data): 3,39,583 private unaided recognised schools; 39,62,114 teachers in them (about 39% of India's 1.01 crore teachers). — [Education for All in India (Arun C. Mehta) PDF](https://educationforallinindia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/UDISEPlus-all-india-national-key-indicators-2020-21-to-2025-26-aruncmehta.pdf); [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2025/09/22/mapping-indias-10-million-teachers-in-six-charts)
- From 2026 CBSE holds Class 10 boards twice a year (best score kept); papers reported as ~50% competency-based, 20% MCQ, 30% descriptive (split varies by source). — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/education/exams/story/cbse-2026-board-exams-heres-what-majorly-changes-for-class-10-and-class-12-students-under-nep-2020-515841-2026-02-12)
- Teachers quoted (Oct 2025, anecdotal): two exams mean double invigilation, corrections and re-teaching; designing competency-based formats "takes time and training"; evaluation quality suffers under the load. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-board-exam-2026-class-10-12-two-chance-date-sheet-school-teachers-double-burden-students-anxiety-subject-marks-syllabus-nep)
- CBSE introduced On-Screen Marking for Class 12 in 2026 (Class 10 stays manual); Delhi government school teachers asked for it to be put on hold for lack of training; after a results row CBSE stated "AI was not used" in evaluation. — [Business Today Feb 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/education/exams/story/cbse-rolls-out-on-screen-marking-for-class-12-boards-answer-sheets-no-change-for-class-10-515369-2026-02-10); [Business Today May 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/ai-was-not-used-cbse-defends-digital-marking-amid-class-12-result-row-531878-2026-05-17); [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-on-screen-marking-system-osm-class-12-board-exams-2026-delhi-government-school-teachers-ask-board-for-phased-roll-out/amp)
- School ERP pricing (vendor pages, unverified): typical ₹100-500 per student per year; low-end TACHY ₹49/student/year or flat ₹4,900/year under 100 students; Schoolites report-card software ₹10/student/month + ₹3,000 registration; small-school totals ₹20,000-1,00,000/year. — [TACHY](https://tachy.in/pricing.php); [Schoolites](https://schoolites.com/student-report-card-software); [EduTinker](https://edutinker.com/?p=39687)
- AI lesson-plan / HPC narrative offerings exist as vendor add-ons: Scolit sells fixed-price projects at ₹1.5-5 lakh per solution to K-12 chains with AI-drafted lesson plans and HPC narratives; NeevLearn markets AI report-card remarks and claims teachers spend 2-4 hours per class per term on remarks (vendor claim). — [Scolit](https://scolit.pages.dev/); [NeevBoard](https://neevboard.pages.dev/features/ai-report-cards)
- A school-ERP incumbent of note: MyClassboard FY25 revenue ₹25.9 crore, PAT ₹10.1 lakh (thin profit after many years). — [Indian Startup Times](https://www.indianstartuptimes.com/?p=26424)

### Inferences
- The buyer in a low-fee private school is the owner/trustee, pricing is per-student-per-year, and the purchase is bundled into ERP; a standalone AI tool has to displace or plug into an ERP the owner already paid for. Sales cycles were not documented in sources, but per-student annual pricing tied to the academic year implies one buying window (roughly Jan-Apr).
- The fresh 2026 pain (competency-based questions; two board exams) is real but is also the pain tutors and coaching centres feel, and they decide in a day rather than a term. A CBQ generator sold to tutors who teach Class 9-10 can later be sold to schools through those same teachers.
- CBSE's public "AI was not used" stance signals reputational sensitivity around AI marking in formal schooling; AI-assisted checking is safer positioned as "teacher's draft, teacher approves" and in low-stakes coaching tests.

### Gaps
- No evidence found (either way) on Indian school teachers personally paying for AI tools; free ChatGPT/Gemini and free government tools (DIKSHA etc.) are the likely substitute. Unverified.
- No data on school software sales-cycle length or who signs; no count of "low-fee" (budget) private schools specifically.
- HPC adoption status by board/state not researched.

## 3. AI checking of handwritten answer sheets for coaching test series

### Takeaway
Supply exists but is enterprise-shaped and quote-only: Eklavvya (universities, boards, large coaching), CrazyGoldfish, and newer small entrants like ClassPulse. Nobody publishes a rupee price per sheet, and independent accuracy data is absent. The one place with public prices is UPSC Mains answer evaluation, where human checking costs ₹100-200 per answer and AI apps charge ~₹999/year, which shows both demand and how fast AI collapses the price.

### Cited Findings
- ClassPulse: AI that checks handwritten answer sheets for Indian coaching centres and schools (Classes 9-12), question by question with teacher approval; first paper free; charged per page checked, "not per student, not per seat"; price on request. GoodFirms shows the company as founded 2026. — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/classpulse); [GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/software/classpulse)
- Eklavvya: AI grading for coaching institutes and schools; pricing "no upfront investment, per booklet, based on pages"; no rupee figure public. Claims ~95% AI-to-human agreement (vendor claim, study unnamed), 31% less grading time per response and 33% per sheet. — [Eklavvya coaching page](https://www.eklavvya.com/ai-grading-coaching-institute/); [Eklavvya AI checking](https://www.eklavvya.com/ai-answer-sheet-checking/); [Eklavvya blog](https://www.eklavvya.com/blog/ai-answer-checking/)
- CrazyGoldfish Technologies is listed on the government IndiaAI AIKosh portal as an AI assessment engine that "reduces evaluation time by up to 60%" in subjective assessments. — [AIKosh](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/use-cases/details/crazygoldfish_technologies_ai_powered_assessment_engine_reduces_evaluation_time_by_up_to_60_in_subjective_learning_assessments.html)
- Other names surfaced without pricing: IntelGrader, GoGrade AI, EduSync AI (university incubation project). — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/intelgrader/about); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/gograde-ai?launch=gograde-ai); [BBDU](https://bbdu.ac.in/edusync-ai/)
- UPSC Mains benchmark: human evaluation cited at ₹100-200 per answer vs AI evaluation inside a ₹999/year plan (AI vendor's own comparison, unverified); Likhit offers 5 free AI evaluations per week; Prayas AI was free for Mains 2025; full human test series cost ₹14,999 (CollectorBabu, 13 tests), ₹26,000 (Insights), ₹28,000 (Vajiram & Ravi). Guides say turnaround over 10 days "kills the feedback loop". — [Prepairo](https://prepairo.ai/upsc/llms/upsc-ai-mains-evaluation/); [Likhit listing](https://www.stork.ai/en/likhit); [Vajiram & Ravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/sure-shot); [CollectorBabu](https://collectorbabu.com/upsc-mains-test-series-2026); [SuperKalam pricing](https://superkalam.com/pricing)
- CBSE's own Class 12 digital marking in 2026 did not use AI and still drew a results controversy. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/ai-was-not-used-cbse-defends-digital-marking-amid-class-12-result-row-531878-2026-05-17)

### Inferences
- Small centres (under ~300 students) are not visibly served: every vendor is quote-based, which normally means a sales call and a minimum volume. A self-serve "photograph the sheets on WhatsApp, get marks + per-question feedback + a parent-ready report, pay ₹2-5 a page" product has no public-priced competitor in the sources found. ClassPulse is the closest and very new.
- Technical risk is real: handwriting in Hindi/regional scripts, maths steps, diagrams. Vendors' ~95% claims are unaudited. A solo product should keep the teacher in the loop (approve/override) and target subjects where vision-LLMs do well (English, SST, science theory, short answers) before maths.
- UPSC AI evaluation is already crowded and racing to free; school-board subjective tests (Class 9-12 CBSE/state) for tuition centres are less contested.
- This is the niche where the reader's existing "AI study app" engineering transfers most directly, but pointed at a payer (the centre) instead of a student.

### Gaps
- No per-sheet rupee price from any Indian vendor; needs quote requests.
- No independent accuracy study on Indian handwritten scripts.
- Unknown whether any vendor has small coaching centres as paying customers at scale.

## 4. Exam-adjacent paid services (admission counselling, study-abroad agents, form-filling, scholarships)

### Takeaway
Families pay striking sums for counselling guidance: ₹2,000 alert packs up to ₹15,000-50,000 for NEET counselling and ₹50,000+GST for engineering, on top of ₹199-999 predictor tools from big brands. It is a trust-heavy, scam-tainted, seasonal market. The solo-developer opening is selling tooling to the small counsellors (B2B) rather than competing for parents against PW, Allen, Careers360 and Adda247.

### Cited Findings
- NEET counselling packages (provider listings, 2025-26): NEET Kaka Ji "Pro Max" ₹15,000 (list ₹30,000); neetugguidance.in ₹2,000 alert package, ₹25,000 counselling, ₹50,000 premium; V4EduSolution MBBS/BDS plan ₹50,000; Bodmas UP NEET package ₹35,500; Adda247 sells a NEET counselling batch with 1:1 counsellor and form-filling support. — [NEET Kaka Ji](https://neetkakajee.com/courses/95-neet-paid-counselling-2025-pro-max-neet-counselling); [neetugguidance.in](https://www.neetugguidance.in/online-booking.php); [V4EduSolution](https://v4edusolution.com/packages); [Bodmas](https://bodmaseducation.com/up-neet-ug-counselling-registration-date-fees-choice-filling-college-list-required-documents); [Adda247](https://www.adda247.com/product-onlineliveclasses/92850/neet-ug-batch-counselling-online-coaching)
- Engineering (JoSAA/CSAB/state): Bodmas premium engineering counselling ₹50,000 + GST, NRI ₹75,000 + GST; RankToCollege tiers ₹199 / ₹499 / ₹999; PW Career Wallah counselling masterclass ₹199 with predictor locked behind batch purchase; Allen sells JEE Main counselling; Careers360 predictor historically ₹499-599 **[pre-2025, user-reported]**. JoSAA itself charges nothing for registration/choice filling (seat acceptance fee ₹30,000 General). — [Bodmas](https://bodmaseducation.com/paid-guidance-engineering); [RankToCollege](https://ranktocollege.com/); [Career Wallah](https://careerwallah.pw.live/jee/details); [Allen](https://pay.allen.in/jee-main/counselling); [Careers360 Q&A](https://www.careers360.com/question-jee-mains-college-predictor-at-599-what-facilities-are-available-in-this-paid-councelling); [Cracku](https://cracku.in/josaa-2026-counselling-fee)
- Trust problems: agents demanding "seat booking" money before counselling; a Hyderabad doctor paid ₹13.9 lakh for a fake allotment letter; ED found large-scale forged NRI-quota certificates (reported 18,000 seats); NEET PG aspirants got calls from people who already knew their ranks and preferred branches. — [Edufever](https://www.edufever.com/neet-ug-nri-admission-scam-alert-2025-10-warnings-ed-cbi); [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/amp/state-news/telangana/hyderabad-gynaecologist-duped-of-lakhs-in-mbbs-admission-scam-165460); [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/mbbs-admission-scam-exposed-18000-neet-ug-pg-seats-filled-using-forged-nri-quota-certificates-ed-reports-mcc-indian-embassy); [The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/education/neet-pg-data-breach-aspirants-scam-calls-counselling-227316)
- Study abroad: consulting market ~USD 537.7 million in 2025 with university commissions 57% of value; student departures fell to 626,000 in 2025 from 908,000 in 2023 (paid-report summary). Commissions 10-15% of first-year tuition, or USD 2,000-6,500 per student in US contracts; consultants also charge students ₹50,000 to ₹4.5 lakh. Funded players: Leverage Edu, Yocket, GradRight, GyanDhan; AI entrant AbroBot.ai claims 10,000 students in 2025 (company PR). — [Credence Research](https://www.credenceresearch.com/report/india-overseas-education-consulting-market); [GradPilot](https://gradpilot.com/news/universities-pay-indian-agents-commission-loophole); [GyanDhan](https://www.gyandhan.com/blogs/others/study-abroad-consultant-commission-bias-university-shortlist); [TechCrunch Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/01/visa-crackdowns-are-blocking-students-study-abroad-dreams-so-indias-leverage-edu-is-rerouting-them/); [Tribune (PR)](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/abrobot-ai-emerges-as-one-of-indias-best-ai-powered-platforms-for-abroad-education-counseling/)
- Form filling: official CSC service charges for NIOS forms are ₹10-71 per form; MCA approved ₹150 per e-form **[undated/older]**; private cyber centres in Karnataka charged ₹100-150 per scheme application (reported as overcharging) **[pre-2025]**. CSC count cited as "more than 1.5 lakh" (non-official source). CSCs are the main offline channel for National Scholarship Portal applications. — [NIOS](https://rcallahabad.nios.ac.in/page/faq-csc-table.html); [Star of Mysore](https://starofmysore.com/cyber-centres-swindle-gruha-jyothi-applicants/amp); [YouthPower](https://youthpower.in/institution/csc)

### Inferences
- Software-plus-human models already exist at the top (Adda247, PW, Allen bundle counsellors with predictors). The long tail of independent counsellors charging ₹15,000-50,000 run on Excel cutoff sheets, WhatsApp groups and Telegram; a white-label "counsellor workbench" (rank-to-college shortlist from past cutoffs, choice-list generator, deadline alerts to each family on WhatsApp, document checklist) is a B2B product at perhaps ₹5,000-20,000 per season. No such vendor surfaced in search, which is weak evidence of emptiness, not proof.
- Risks: extreme seasonality (roughly June-October), cutoff data correctness is liability-bearing, and the category's scam reputation rubs off. Study-abroad is shrinking in volume and crowded with funded startups: avoid.
- Cyber-cafe form filling is priced at ₹10-150 a form: too little money per transaction, operators are price-takers, and government portals change constantly. Avoid as a first product.

### Gaps
- Number of independent admission counsellors in India: not found.
- Scholarship agents' charges: not found.
- No evidence on whether counsellors pay for software today.

## 5. Non-education small-business niches with proof of paying for software

### Takeaway
Small Indian businesses demonstrably pay in a few lanes: billing/accounting (Vyapar ~₹3,400/yr, Tally ₹22,500 licence), WhatsApp API tools (₹999-3,500/month), and vertical apps for clinics, gyms, salons and brokers (₹800-2,000/month). The professional-services niche with the clearest numbers and document-heavy pain is CA firms: 1,00,138 registered firms, 72,696 of them sole proprietors, already buying practice-management and now AI notice-reply tools at about ₹3,000/month.

### Inferences
- **Is B2B/B2B2C the better path?** Yes on the evidence available. Every rupee price found in education software is paid by someone who earns from students (tutor, centre, school, counsellor); the only consumer-paid items are high-stakes one-offs (counselling, UPSC evaluation). The consumer study-app the reader built competes with free; its engineering (question generation, answer evaluation) is the core of niches #1 and #2.
- **Recommended wedge:** one product for niches #1 + #2 + #3 combined, for a narrow starting segment such as "Class 9-10 CBSE and one state board, science/SST/English tuition centres with 30-200 students in one city/language". Tutor uploads chapter list, gets a blueprint-matched test with competency-based questions; students write on paper; tutor photographs sheets; AI drafts marks and feedback for approval; parents get a WhatsApp report with the centre's branding. Price as an annual plan in the ₹5,000-12,000 band plus usage for checking. The parent report is the retention hook and doubles as the tutor's marketing, which addresses the acquisition pain in #4.
- **Why a solo developer can win here when Classplus could not:** the funded model needed field sales to push a ₹20-30k app-builder; a narrow utility sold over WhatsApp with self-serve onboarding has no such cost. The risk shifts from sales cost to differentiation against free ChatGPT, which is why the handwritten-checking and parent-report loop (which generic chatbots do not package) matters more than question generation alone.
- **Validation before building:** none of the willingness-to-pay for #2 at centre level is proven in sources. Run 15-20 interviews and a concierge pilot (check one real test for free, by hand-driving the model) before writing product code.
- **Solo-clone signal:** searches for tutor apps, GST notice agents, legal drafting, clinic and school tools all returned products on vercel.app, netlify.app, pages.dev and lovable.app subdomains. Many solo developers are attacking the same "obvious" vertical ideas in 2026; distribution inside a specific community, not the build, is the scarce asset.

### Gaps
- The "empty" judgments rest on absence of search results, which is weak evidence; a niche may have local players invisible to English-language web search.
- No first-hand buyer quotes (Reddit, Quora, Play Store) were retrieved for any niche.
- Counts are missing for most non-education niches (gyms, salons, clinics, brokers, counsellors).
- inc42.com could not be fetched in this session; the Classplus pivot details should be re-read at source once that host is allowed.
