# Unmet, painful problems of Indian exam students and parents (as of 11 Nov 2026)

Method note for the report writer: findings below come from ~28 web searches (search-result summaries, not full-page reads). Where a number came from an aggregator, coaching blog or a vendor's own page, it is labelled. I could NOT retrieve Reddit threads (r/JEENEETards, r/CBSE, r/Indian_Academia), Quora answers, YouTube comments or Play Store review pages directly — the search tool returned none. Student-voice evidence is therefore from news quotes, Careers360 Q&A threads, Trustpilot and consumer-complaint sites. Treat "Reddit says" claims as NOT verified by this research.

Already built by the reader and deliberately excluded: flashcards/quizzes from notes, AI mentor, mistake notebook, spaced repetition, answer-writing coach, confidence check, focus tracker, habit coach, weekly WhatsApp family update, Hindi/English parent explainers, daily puzzle, interest-based rewording.

## 1. Sizes: how many candidates per exam (2025 and 2026)

### Takeaway
The addressable crowd is very large and concentrated in a few dated events: about 43-46 lakh CBSE board students, 13-15 lakh JEE Main, about 22.8 lakh NEET registrants, 15.7 lakh CUET registrants, 28 lakh SSC CGL applicants and 1.2 crore RRB NTPC applicants. Each has a hard calendar, so demand for "what do I do now" help arrives in sharp, predictable spikes.

### Inferences
- The NEET 2026 gap between 22.05 lakh appeared (3 May) and 19.99 lakh appeared (result data) is most likely first sitting vs. the 21 June re-exam; that would mean roughly 2 lakh candidates did not return for the re-exam. This is my reading, not stated by a source.
- The average coaching spend (₹1,800-4,000 a year) is tiny compared with the ₹25,000-50,000 counselling packages in section 3. The willingness to pay is concentrated in a small window (results to admission), not spread across the study year.
- Government-job aspirants (SSC + railways) outnumber JEE+NEET by several times and are older, self-paying and mostly Hindi-belt, but have far lower ability to pay per head (inference from exam counts only).

### Gaps
- No unique-candidate total for JEE Main 2026 across both sessions was found (predictor sites assume 15.5-16.5 lakh; see section 2).
- No 2026 applicant counts found for SSC CGL 2026, SSC CHSL/GD, IBPS PO/Clerk or state boards (UP, Bihar, Maharashtra).
- Official NTA/CBSE press notes were not opened directly; figures are via news sites.

## 2. Which moments in the exam year create the most anxiety, confusion or spending?

### Takeaway
The sharpest unmet pains are not "studying" pains; they are high-stakes admin and decision moments where one mistake is irreversible: counselling/choice filling (JoSAA, CSAB, state engineering, MCC + 30-odd state NEET counsellings), re-evaluation and result disputes, form and document errors, exam-centre and cancellation chaos, and fake notices. 2026 made these worse: NEET UG was cancelled and re-held, and CBSE's first on-screen marking triggered a re-evaluation crisis.

### Cited Findings

**Pain A — Counselling and choice filling after JEE (JoSAA / CSAB / state counsellings)**
- Who/how many: 10-13 lakh JEE Main candidates per session (section 1), plus state-CET candidates.
- JoSAA choice filling is effectively one-shot: guides say choices cannot be added, removed or reordered in later rounds; only Freeze/Float/Slide can be changed — [jeecounselling.in](https://jeecounselling.in/josaa-2026-round-3-float-or-freeze-decision), [CollegeDekho](https://collegedekho.com/articles/tips-to-fill-choices-in-josaa-counseling)
- Guides themselves conflict on basic rules (whether Slide exists, whether the response window is 24 hours or 2-3 days) — [Careers360 explainer](https://news.careers360.com/josaa-counselling-2026-freeze-float-closing-rank-and-other-key-terms-explained-registration-round-1-seat-allotment-iit-nit-iiit/amp), [engineeringhulk](https://engineeringhulk.com/jee/josaa-counselling/freeze-float-slide/)
- "Unplanned" choice ordering is listed as a major common mistake — [Careers360](https://engineering.careers360.com/articles/major-mistakes-avoid-in-josaa-counselling)
- Students ask the same Freeze/Float question year after year on Careers360 Q&A (e.g. "if we choose float and don't get a higher preference, do we keep the seat?") — [Careers360 Q&A](https://www.careers360.com/question-if-we-choose-float-option-and-if-in-next-rounds-we-dont-get-higher-preference-seat-then-can-we-still-have-that-seat-which-we-got-before-choosing-the-float-option)
- No central counselling across states/private institutes: a consumer complaint describes a student who waited for COMEDK round 3 alongside MHT-CET CAP rounds and could not get a refund (single user complaint, unverified) — [ConsumerComplaints.in](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/comedk-refund-of-fees-c3091112)
- Hidden/extra fees after allotment: Maharashtra CET Cell warned engineering colleges in 2026 against charging unapproved fees — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/mht-cet-cap-2026-maharashtra-cell-warns-engineering-btech-colleges-illegal-extra-fees-admission-cancellation); Telangana private engineering colleges reported flouting fee norms (May 2026) — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/several-private-engineering-colleges-flout-fee-norms-in-telangana)
- Management-quota advances: some colleges take up to half the course fee in advance and deduct 10-50% on refund (older report, pre-2025) — [DNA](https://dnaindia.com/bangalore/report-booking-an-engineering-seat-conditions-mostly-unsaid-apply-1579879/amp)
- Workaround today: free YouTube/coaching blogs, free IIT Kanpur SATHEE counselling guides ([SATHEE](https://sathee.iitk.ac.in/counselling-guides)), college predictors, and paid consultants (prices in section 3).
- Why free ChatGPT/Gemini struggle: needs the exact current-year rules, category/quota/home-state/gender-specific opening and closing ranks for each round, and deadlines that change by notice. Even specialist sites contradict each other on rules (above), so a general model has no reliable ground truth unless it is fed verified official data.

**Pain B — NEET counselling: MCC plus every state, bonds, penalties, fraud**
- Who/how many: 11,21,185 NEET UG 2026 qualifiers — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/neet-ug-2026-result-highlights-absentees-surge-qualifiers-fall-cut-offs-rise-toppers-list)
- Each state runs its own counselling with its own domicile, reservation, fee and eligibility rules; state counselling covers 85% state quota in government colleges and 100% of private seats in the state — [Careers360](https://medicine.careers360.com/articles/neet-state-counselling-2026), [Emversity](https://emversity.com/blog/neet/state-counselling-neet-ug-2026)
- Two different money traps are routinely confused: the service bond and the seat-discontinuation penalty — [Careers360](https://medicine.careers360.com/articles/state-wise-neet-ug-bond-types-penalty-amount). Example sizes: Chhattisgarh ₹25 lakh bond for government MBBS seats (2025) — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/chhattisgarh-neet-ug-2025-rs-25-lakh-bond-policy-mandatory-for-government-mbbs-seats-merit-list-on-august-6-medical-colleges/amp)
- Private colleges overcharging: Rajasthan government flagged private colleges for higher MBBS fees under a 15% "unapproved" management quota and ordered refunds with 12% interest (NEET 2025) — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/rajasthan-neet-2025-govt-flags-private-colleges-for-charging-higher-mbbs-fees-15-unapproved-management-quota-refund-12-interest)
- Fraud is constant: 3 students lost ₹18.58 lakh to people posing as NEET counsellors found via Instagram (Mumbai, 2026) — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/mumbai-crime-3-students-duped-of-1858-lakh-in-fake-mbbs-bams-admission-scam-powai-police-register-case); Haryana man lost ₹25 lakh (2026) — [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/state-news/haryana/haryana-man-duped-of-rs-25-lakh-in-mbbs-admission-fraud-2-booked-165135); Hyderabad police issued a NEET 2026 advisory on MBBS admission scams — [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/amp/state-news/telangana/neet-2026-alert-hyderabad-police-warns-of-mbbs-admission-scams-169790); CBI arrested two (including an admission consultant) for allegedly charging ₹87.5 lakh per student on a false promise to manipulate NEET UG 2025 scores — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/cbi-nab-two-for-charging-rs-87-5-lakh-per-student-to-manipulate-neet-ug-2025-scores)
- 2026 timeline shock: NEET UG held 3 May was cancelled on 12 May over paper-leak allegations, CBI probe ordered, re-exam on 21 June — [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/breaking-news/neet-ug-2026-cancelled-paper-leak-cbi-probe), [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/amp/story/current-affairs%2Fneet-ug-2026-to-be-held-again-on-21-june-after-cancellation-of-exam-over-allegations-of-question-paper-leak); state counselling registrations shifted to late July-August — [neet2seat (specialist tracker, unverified)](https://neet2seat.com/news/haryana-neet-ug-2026-round-1-registration)
- Workaround today: paid consultants at ₹25,000-50,000+ (section 3), Telegram/YouTube channels, relatives.
- Why free AI struggles: 30+ state portals, PDFs in English/regional languages, rules reissued every year, stakes of ₹10-25 lakh per wrong move; parents need a verified, dated, state-specific answer and a deadline tracker, not a plausible paragraph.

- Students alleged marks were reduced without requests; Supreme Court declined to order the portal reopened — [Kerala Kaumudi](https://keralakaumudi.com/en/india/general/cbse-class-12-re-evaluation-under-scanner-as-students-allege-marks-reduced-without-requests-1770682), [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-osm-re-evaluation-system-sc-declines-direct-reopening-portal-class-12-answer-sheets-results-supreme-court-students-website/amp)
- Supplementary exam: students alleged chapter-wise weightage was ignored and sought grace marks — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-class-12-supplementary-controversy-exam-tough-no-chapter-wise-weightage-students-demand-grace-marks/amp)
- Workaround today: school teachers, YouTube explainers, tele-counselling from CBSE ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/cbse-extends-tele-counselling-support-for-class-12-students-amid-concerns-over-on-screen-marking-evaluation)); no evidence found of a paid service.
- Why free AI struggles: the student needs to compare their own scanned answer book against the official marking scheme, step by step, and decide which questions are worth challenging within a short paid window. That is a structured workflow on the student's own document plus the official scheme, with a deadline — not a chat.

- Why free AI struggles: needs minute-level freshness and a check against the official source's own notice list; a general chatbot may repeat the rumour or lag behind it.

- Why free AI struggles: this is a human-network and live-data problem (who else is going to the same centre, is that centre cancelled today, where to stay cheaply), not a knowledge problem.

**Pain H — The drop-year and dummy-school decisions**
- CBSE's 75% attendance rule bars dummy-school students from Class 12 boards from 2025-26; CBSE has acted against about 300 dummy schools — [Organiser](https://organiser.org/2025/03/28/284791/education/cbse-makes-75-per-cent-attendance-mandatory-to-appear-for-class-12-board-exam-non-attending-students-to-be-barred/amp/), [India CSR](https://indiacsr.in/cbse-bans-dummy-school-students-from-board-exams-what-it-means-for-class-12)
- Delhi (March 2026) and Gujarat (June 2026) started crackdowns on dummy admissions — [Ezyschooling](https://ezyschooling.com/news/delhi-dummy-admissions-class-11-and-12), [Vibes of India](https://www.vibesofindia.com/?p=380889)
- Centre formed a committee (June 2025) on coaching dependency, dummy schools and JEE/NEET pressure — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/centre-forms-committee-tackle-jee-neet-coaching-dependenc-dummy-schools-cbse-exam-pressure-suicides-regulations-iit-nit-kv-jnv)
- Why free AI struggles: the drop decision needs honest base rates (how many repeaters at this score improved enough), which no one publishes; advice today comes from coaching sales teams who profit from a "yes".

### Inferences
- Ranking by "irreversible + expensive + time-boxed + rules change every year", the strongest wedges are: (1) NEET state-by-state counselling navigator with bond/penalty/fee truth table and deadline alerts; (2) JoSAA/CSAB/state engineering choice-list builder and round-by-round Freeze/Float decision helper; (3) CBSE answer-book re-evaluation helper (compare own scanned copy to marking scheme, decide what to challenge); (4) verified-notice checker ("is this circular real?") in Hindi and English; (5) document locker with certificate-expiry and correction-window alerts.
- Wedges (1)-(3) are seasonal (May-September). Wedge (4) and (5) recur across the year and across exams, which fits the "come back weekly" goal better; (1)-(2) fit the "pay once" goal better.
- A verified-notice checker is naturally shareable on WhatsApp (the same channel the rumours travel on) and would fit the reader's existing family-update feature without duplicating it.
- Rank predictors and PYQ analysis are saturated with free tools from large players; weak wedge.
- The fraud pattern (fake counsellors found on Instagram) means trust is the product. A solo developer cannot out-shout consultants, but can be the one who shows the official source link and date next to every answer and never promises a seat.
- CBSE's two-board-exam format for Class 10 (6.68 lakh took the second exam) creates a brand-new, unanswered question for families every May: "should my child sit the second exam, in which subjects?" No evidence was found of any tool for this.

### Gaps
- No direct Reddit/Quora/YouTube evidence was retrieved; the pains are evidenced by news and Q&A sites instead.
- No sourced evidence found on practical/viva exam pain or on handwriting/presentation in board answers (both suggested by the brief). They remain unverified hypotheses.
- No count found of students who lose seats to choice-filling errors, or of forms rejected for photo/signature errors.
- No data on cyber-cafe form-filling charges.
- No base-rate data on drop-year outcomes.
- CUET-specific confusion in 2026 is thinly evidenced (one court document, one delay report). The best sourced CUET complaint (DU missing from the university list) is from 2024, older than 2025 — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/cuet-ug-application-form-2024-confusing-delhi-university-unavailable-in-list-of-universities-claim-aspirants-parents)

## 3. What do students and parents pay for around these moments, and at what prices?

### Takeaway
Counselling guidance is the clearest proven paid job: NEET consultants openly list ₹25,000-50,000+ (plus GST), engineering consultants ₹11,800-50,000, while a low-priced tier of ₹249-3,999 also exists. That spread suggests room for a trustworthy ₹199-999 self-serve product. Official fees for the same processes are small.

### Cited Findings
- Engineering counselling (vendor's own page): ₹25,000 + GST basic; premium ₹50,000 (or ₹20,000 + ₹30,000 instalments) — [BODMAS Education](https://bodmaseducation.com/counselling-guidance/engineering-counselling); ₹11,800 for JoSAA + CSAB guidance — [BODMAS Education](https://bodmaseducation.com/csab-counselling)
- Low-end engineering counselling (small/unknown vendors, unverified quality): ₹999 for NIT/IIT-level counselling, ₹3,999 for all counselling processes — [College Network](https://collegenetwork.duckdns.org/); ₹249 JoSAA + CSAB package (offer marked expired) — [Collage Predictor](https://collage-predictor.netlify.app/counselling)
- NEET UG counselling: ₹35,500 for UP NEET UG 2026 counselling, with a statement that a seat cannot be guaranteed — [BODMAS Education](https://bodmaseducation.com/up-neet-ug-counselling-registration-date-fees-choice-filling-college-list-required-documents); "exclusive" service ₹50,000 onwards + 18% GST, NRI service from ₹1,00,000 — [NEET Navigator](https://www.neetnavigator.com/neet-ug); Alert package ₹2,000 (another page says ₹5,000), Counselling ₹25,000, Premium ₹50,000 — [neetugguidance.in](https://www.neetugguidance.in/online-booking.php), [same vendor's services page](https://www.neetugguidance.in/services.php)

### Inferences
- Because premium general AI is free in India, "better explanations" or "AI tutor" has near-zero paid value. Payment evidence exists only where there is verified local data plus a deadline plus an irreversible outcome (counselling).
- The free ChatGPT Go year that began 4 Nov 2025 would be ending around now (Nov 2026) for early sign-ups; whether OpenAI extended it was not checked.
- A price ladder is visible: ₹249-999 (self-serve tools), ₹2,000-5,000 (alerts only), ₹11,800-50,000 (human hand-holding). "Alerts only" selling for ₹2,000-5,000 is notable: it means deadline/notice tracking alone is a paid product in NEET counselling.
- All prices above are vendors' own list prices; no data on how many people actually buy.

### Gaps
- No prices found for topper/senior 1:1 mentorship sessions, test series, or paid doubt-solving in this research.
- No market-size estimate for counselling consultancy.
- Careers360 companion prices not retrieved.
- No RedSeer/Bain/Inc42/The Ken data retrieved on edtech market size for 2026.

## 4. What do students complain about in existing apps and edtech?

### Takeaway
Documented complaints centre on sales pressure, refunds, spam calls and reliability, not on content. Evidence about AI doubt-solver accuracy and notification overload was not found, so those remain assumptions.

### Cited Findings
- PW's own DRHP: student complaints and queries rose 42% to 10 lakh in FY25 from 7.4 lakh — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/can-physicswallah-fix-its-teacher-problem)

### Inferences
- A product that visibly does the opposite (no phone number required, no sales calls, pay-per-use with instant refund, no fake topper claims) has a real positioning advantage; these are cheap for a solo developer and expensive for a sales-led company.

- Nothing specific found on Allen or Doubtnut complaints.

## 5. Which needs are unserved for Hindi-medium, regional-language, tier-2/3 and low-end Android users?

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin but consistent: Hindi-medium students face translation errors in papers and thinner prep material, and the largest Hindi-belt crowds (SSC, railways) bear the worst logistics. I found no hard data on device or data constraints.

### Cited Findings
- JEE Main 2025 (older than 2026 but within 2025): teachers cited translation errors; a record 12 questions dropped — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/education/nta-under-fire-once-again-after-a-record-12-questions-dropped-in-jee-main-2025-what-went-wrong/2494480)
- NEET 2026: Hindi-medium candidates at a Jharkhand (Palamu) centre were given English papers; about 4-hour delay; inquiry ordered — [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/amp/state-news/jharkhand/neet-2026-delayed-over-4-hours-at-jharkhand-exam-centre-after-question-paper-language-mix-up-170077)
- OLDER THAN 2025: NEET 2021 Hindi translation of a Physics question omitted a term; Supreme Court dismissed the plea — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/neet-ug-2021-supreme-court-dismisses-plea-seeking-omission-of-physics-question-error-hindi-version)
- A coaching blog says NEET question banks, test series, video lectures and doubt-solving are overwhelmingly English and Hindi-medium books beyond NCERT are thin (commercial source, indicative only) — [KSquare Institute](https://ksquareinstitute.in/blog/?p=6339)
- CUET UG 2026: English had 12.64 lakh registrations while regional-language subjects saw low uptake — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/cuet-ug-2026-result-english-leads-with-1264-lakh-registrations-regional-language-subjects-see-lower-uptake)
- Rural vs urban spend: ₹1,793 vs ₹3,988 per student per year on coaching (MoSPI 2025) — [PIB release copy](https://educationforallinindia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/PIB-Press-Release-26-08-2025-NSSO-Comrephensive-Modular-Survey-on-Education.pdf)
- Gender gap: families spend more on boys for private school and coaching (MoSPI 2025) — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/mospi-survey-families-spend-more-boys-private-school-coaching-tuition-fees-cms-education-2025-nsso-data-gender)
- Government-job aspirants travelled about 500 km to centres and found exams cancelled (2025 and 2026) — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/ssc-mts-exam-cancelled-across-8-states-multiple-centres-fresh-admit-cards-whats-happening-anxious-aspirants-new-dates)
- Free official help exists but is English/web heavy: IIT Kanpur SATHEE publishes counselling guides — [SATHEE](https://sathee.iitk.ac.in/counselling-guides); askIITM offers a JoSAA explainer in English, Hindi, Telugu and Tamil — [askIITM](https://askiitm.com/insightful-reads/4_how-does-the-josaa-counselling-and-choice-filling-process-work/)

### Inferences
- A bilingual "English and Hindi side by side" view of a question, flagging where the two versions differ, is a small, concrete tool for Hindi-medium JEE/NEET/SSC students that builds on the reader's existing rewording feature without repeating it. Evidence of demand is indirect (recurring translation-error disputes), not measured.
- Counselling, scholarship and re-evaluation notices are issued as English PDFs; parents in Hindi-belt and regional-language households are the decision makers and payers. A verified notice explained in the parent's language with the official link is a gap that general AI can partly fill but does not push proactively or verify.
- For SSC/railway aspirants, the unmet need is logistics and trustworthy status (is my exam on, where is my centre, who is travelling with me), which suits a light WhatsApp-first tool rather than a heavy app.

### Gaps
- No ASER, RedSeer or Bain data retrieved on device type, data cost or language preference of exam students in 2025-26.
- No evidence on Marathi/Tamil/Telugu/Bengali-medium needs specifically.
- No data on what share of JEE/NEET candidates choose Hindi or regional-language papers in 2026.
- No Play Store evidence on app size/data complaints.
